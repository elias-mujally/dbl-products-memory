## Acceptance protocol for the highest-risk gates

**G02 — required scope:** input captures, active state including control/source/snapshot SQLite and sidecars, source archive, run/audit logs, exported reports, backups and restored copies. The package folder needs integrity against untrusted mutation; data folders additionally require privacy. Inspect child files after approved synthetic creation, not merely root ownership. Resolve effective group/inheritance permissions; a deny for one account alone does not prove every principal is excluded. SYSTEM/Administrators and approved operator are the declared trusted boundary; this does not protect against administrators, a compromised same-user process or malware. NTFS ACL privacy is neither encryption at rest nor exfiltration prevention. No secret-content read is needed to test a synthetic canary. A future correction to ACLs/accounts is a separately reviewed change, not an automatic test repair.

**G03 — bounded meaning:** status-read denial stays UNKNOWN/BLOCKED; do not repeat rejected commands, elevate or weaken protection. An allowed Windows Security view may be recorded by the owner. Any scan is a separately approved operation because it may quarantine a working copy. Capture exact pin, scope, start/end time, signature age/result and protection history; scan submission alone is not completion. If SmartScreen, policy, Defender or native-loader blocks operation, preserve the refusal; no exclusion, Unblock-File, policy change, cloud sample submission or bypass. Offline stale signatures cannot qualify current customer readiness; update/connectivity, if needed, needs independent approval on the approved supported target. A clean scan is time-bounded evidence, not a malware-free guarantee. Hashes are not publisher signatures; a controlled private pilot can use separately authenticated pins and owner delivery, while public distribution/signing remains separate.

**G06 — measured outcome:** operator records Windows/account/runtime prerequisites and verifies ZIP before running bundled code; extracts to Arabic/spaced paths, performs3source checks/imports, current/old replay, current5items1450SAR and3history values1470/1470/1450, Arabic report, pinned backup/verification/new-directory restore/reopen/audit. Observe first-attempt errors and self-recovery from the guide. Report review includes browser/version, narrow layout, zoom and unintended disclosure. Deliberately wrong independent expected hash uses a fixture copy and must stop before import; never tamper with Preview1 or existing evidence. Failure/assistance is recorded honestly; a coached successful completion is assisted acceptance, not independent success.
